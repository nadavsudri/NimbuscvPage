Overview

What it does.

One hand picks and strums — pinch like you're holding a pick and sweep across the strings. The other shapes chords by signing 1–5, each mapped from a 12-chord library.

Two hands, two roles, one instrument. Strum patterns come from the picking hand; harmony comes from the chord hand.

Keep the chord hand steady and let the picking hand do the rhythm work.

How to use

Play in four moves.

  1. Add Strings

    Place the Strings instrument on your canvas — six virtual strings appear.

  2. Hold a pick

    With one hand, pinch as if holding a pick.

  3. Strum

    Sweep that hand across the strings to pick or strum.

  4. Shape the chord

    With the other hand, sign a number 1–5 to select a chord from the 12-chord library.
