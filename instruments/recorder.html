Overview

What it does.

Record any hand pose or movement, name it, and map it to a CC channel. Trigger mode for on/off switches, scale mode for continuous control.

Your vocabulary, your rules - build a personal set of gestures the rest of Nimbus can use.

Record a slightly exaggerated pose - it makes the gesture easier to detect reliably.

How to use

Record in four moves.

  1. Hit record

    Open the Gesture Recorder and press Record.

  2. Perform the gesture

    Do the hand pose or movement you want to capture, then stop.

  3. Name & map it

    Give it a name and assign it to a CC channel.

  4. Choose a mode

    Pick Trigger mode for on/off switches or Scale mode for continuous control, then test it live.
