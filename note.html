How it works

Play in four moves.

  1. Plug in

    Add NOTE to your DAW as a plugin and choose your audio input - guitar, mic, or line-in.

  2. Play

    Play your instrument normally. NOTE follows your pitch in real time.

  3. Convert

    Every note you play is turned into a MIDI note and sent out almost instantly.

  4. Drive anything

    Route that MIDI into any synth, sampler, or virtual instrument and let your playing perform it.
